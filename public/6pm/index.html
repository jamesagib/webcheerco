How it works

Set it up once. It runs every night.

  1. Pick your apps

    Choose apps or whole categories with Apple's Screen Time picker.

  2. Go about your day

    Nothing is blocked during the day. Scroll as much as you like until 6.

  3. Get your evening back

    From 6 PM to 6 AM those apps stay asleep, so the evening is yours.

Meet Momo

Momo keeps watch tonight.

Momo is the panda who lives in 6pm. Open the app in the evening and you'll find Momo keeping watch, with a countdown to when social media opens again.
