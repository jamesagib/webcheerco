6pm · App

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

6pm ("the app") is developed by James Agib. This policy explains what information 6pm collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Your Account

6pm asks you to create an account with Sign in with Apple, Google, or an email address and password. We store your name, email address, a sign-in identifier from Apple or Google, and an encrypted password if you use email. To keep you signed in securely, we also store session records that include your IP address and device type. If you choose Apple's "Hide My Email," we receive only the relay address Apple provides.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment card. We use a subscription service provider to show subscription offers and check whether your subscription is active. It receives your account identifier, name, email address, purchase and subscription status, and basic device information such as device model, iOS version, language, and app version.

Product Analytics

We use an analytics service provider, which processes usage events on our behalf in the United States, to understand how people use 6pm, such as where people leave onboarding. Once you pass the age check, 6pm sends it in-app usage events, such as onboarding steps viewed, the subscription offer viewed, onboarding completed, the app opened, and settings turned on or off, along with the time, platform, and app version. These events carry a random identifier created when you install the app; after you sign in, it is linked to your 6pm account ID. We do not send our analytics provider your name, email address, password, payment details, or any Screen Time or app usage data. We do not use analytics for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps and websites, and we never sell it. When you delete your account, new events are no longer linked to it.

Screen Time & Family Controls

6pm uses Apple's Screen Time API to block the apps you choose from 6 PM to 6 AM. Apple handles this on your device. We cannot see which apps you choose, which apps you use, or how long you use them. Any Screen Time reports shown in 6pm are generated by iOS on your device and are never sent to us.

What Stays on Your Device

Your blocked-app selection, schedule, streak, the name you enter during setup, your reason for using 6pm, notification and Live Activity preferences, and your age confirmation are stored only on your iPhone.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, 6pm schedules reminders on your device, such as a heads-up before your evening starts and a morning check-in. These are local notifications; we do not use a push notification service.

Email

We may send account emails, such as a welcome message or a password reset link, to the address on your account. We do not send marketing emails.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to provide your account, deliver and manage your subscription, understand how the app is used so we can improve it, keep the service secure, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Service Providers

We use service providers acting on our behalf: our hosting and infrastructure providers run our servers and database and send account emails, a subscription service provider manages subscription offers, and an analytics service provider processes usage events. They may use this information only to provide their services to us. If you choose Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, or buy a subscription through the App Store, Apple or Google handles that under its own privacy policy.

Keeping and Deleting Your Data

We keep your account information while your account exists. You can delete your account anytime in 6pm under Settings → Delete account. This removes your account, sessions, and sign-in records from our database and revokes Sign in with Apple access, and analytics events are no longer linked to your account. Deleting the app removes the information stored on your device. Canceling a subscription is managed separately in your Apple Account settings.

Age Requirement

6pm is for people 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Choices and Rights

You can access, correct, or delete your information, or ask a question about it, by contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor requests as required.

Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. For significant changes, we will also let you know in the app.
